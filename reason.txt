Everyone speaks row by row.
Everyone is called generation by generation.

Depth



Think of it like this:

Mission:

Start at the root.

Walk until you find the value.

Count every step you take.

When you arrive...

That's the depth.

For example,

        50
       /  \
     30    70
    /
   20

Looking for 20.

Start at 50.

↓

Move to 30.
Step = 1

↓

Move to 20.
Step = 2

Found it.

Depth = 2

That's all.


Height

Height is almost the opposite mission.

Mission:

Find the node.

Now stand on it.

Look down both sides.

Whichever side reaches the furthest leaf...

Count those steps.

That's the height.

      
      What does "balanced" mean?

A tree is balanced if every node satisfies this rule:

The difference between the height of its left subtree 
and the height of its right subtree is no more than 1.

That means:

|leftHeight - rightHeight| <= 1

Notice something important:

It doesn't say the root.

It says every node.
